# Brand Voice — SlimCRM Sales

Use a calm, direct, helpful, and honest tone. Write short sentences and explain technical language in familiar terms. Start from a sourced business context; describe how SlimCRM may relate without claiming an unverified problem. Use accurate, currently verified product facts and state limits. End with one clear, low-pressure next step.

Avoid hype, pressure, invented familiarity, unsupported case studies, claims, pricing, discounts, ROI, implementation dates, or guarantees. Use an appropriate Vietnamese form of address and a sender identity explicitly configured for the active workspace.
